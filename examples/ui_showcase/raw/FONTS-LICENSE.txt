Rich-text demo fonts: DejaVu Sans
=================================

font_dejavu_r.ttf   DejaVu Sans (Regular)
font_dejavu_b.ttf   DejaVu Sans Bold
font_dejavu_i.ttf   DejaVu Sans Oblique
font_dejavu_bi.ttf  DejaVu Sans Bold Oblique

The DejaVu fonts are a public-domain derivative of the Bitstream Vera Fonts.
Redistribution and use (including bundling into application asset packs) is
permitted. Full Cyrillic coverage; used here only for the ui_showcase Rich Text
tab to demonstrate real bold / italic / bold-italic faces.
